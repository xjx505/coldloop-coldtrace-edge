Unite Ideas Instructions Guide — notes

The guide is a four-page visual submission walkthrough.

It covers:
- sign in/register;
- navigate to the Reboot campaign;
- choose "Submit Your Idea";
- read submission guidance;
- select the challenge;
- complete required fields;
- save draft;
- submit;
- continue updating demo/code/details before the deadline where allowed.

No additional PowerPoint layout, pitch-time, typography, or slide-design requirements were found in the guide.
