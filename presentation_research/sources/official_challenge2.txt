Challenge 2: AI for Cold-Chain Monitoring and Food-Loss Reduction

Problem:
Fresh food can lose quality during transportation, storage, and distribution when temperature, humidity, or handling conditions are not properly maintained. In Qatar’s hot climate and import-dependent food system, these disruptions can shorten shelf life, increase costs, and generate avoidable food waste.

Challenge:
Develop an AI-powered tool that combines sensor readings, product information, location and time data, and transportation and storage records to detect cold-chain issues, estimate spoilage risk, and predict remaining shelf life.

The solution should provide timely alerts and recommend practical actions, such as inspecting or rerouting shipments, adjusting storage conditions, prioritizing products for sale, or redirecting surplus food when food-safety requirements allow.
