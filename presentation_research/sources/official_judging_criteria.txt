Judging Criteria (Applicable to All Challenges)

1. Solution Effectiveness and Prototype Readiness: How well the prototype addresses the selected challenge and demonstrates its core functionality.

2. Creativity and Innovation: How original and innovative the team’s approach is compared with existing solutions.

3. Scope and Effort: How appropriate and substantial the team’s work is, considering the hackathon timeframe.

4. Technical Execution and Feasibility: How technically sound, well explained, and feasible the solution is using the available resources and technologies.

5. Communication and Presentation: How clearly and convincingly the team communicates its solution, decisions, and potential impact.

6. Open-Source Alignment: How effectively the solution uses open technologies and enables others to access, reuse, or adapt the work.

7. Sustainability and SDG Alignment: How strongly the solution supports environmental sustainability and relevant UN Sustainable Development Goals.
