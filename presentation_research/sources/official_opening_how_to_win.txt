Reboot The Earth Doha 2026 Opening Ceremony — "How to Win Reboot 2026"

Organizer presentation emphasizes four themes:

1. Use AI Responsibly
   Innovative, ethical and privacy-respecting uses of AI to solve problems that were previously intractable.

2. Relevance to societal challenges
   Addresses a pressing need in society, delivering change in a dimension previously unexplored.

3. Open Source and a Digital Public Good
   Solutions on track to be digital public goods, working toward Sustainable Development Goals and following Digital Principles.

4. Scalable, with real-world impact
   Solutions with a tangible roadmap for growth, public adoption and global impact.
