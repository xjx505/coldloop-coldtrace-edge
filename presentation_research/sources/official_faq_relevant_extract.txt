Reboot The Earth CMU FAQs 2026 — presentation/judging extract

Official FAQ states:

- After the four-day hackathon development period (September 23–26, 2026), teams participate in science fair-style judging.
- Judges select five finalist teams to present their solutions in the final round.
- An expert judging panel selects the overall winner.
- Four additional awards recognize environmental impact, technical excellence, open-source adoption, and creativity.
- Final deliverables may include:
  * platform (dashboard/interface/website/app);
  * code base, appropriately commented/uploaded;
  * documentation: SHORT PRESENTATION (7–10 SLIDES) describing the submission, including approach/methodology;
  * demo video.

The FAQ does not state the exact finalist pitch duration.
