# ColdTrace EDGE-3 model card

**Deployment model:** `coldtrace-edge3-logistic-v1` · regularized class-weighted logistic regression fitted on all six shipments after LOSO · target `y_next_120_R2`. The target is a processed severe thermal-risk state within 120 minutes, not spoilage, food safety, or shelf life.

**Sensors:** Front_Middle, Middle_Middle, Rear_Middle. Temporal features use the mean of available configured probes; spatial features use only those three. A valid inference window requires seven consecutive 10-minute observations over 60 minutes, at least four with usable temperature, at least two current probe positions, and spatial coverage from at least two positions in four of the seven observations. A one-sensor ESP32 cannot use this model; the app remains in warm-up/configuration-mismatch state. The complete feature specification is `edge/feature_schema.json` and the on-device pipeline is `edge/model/edge_model.json`.

**Training:** 2,510 pre-event eligible rows, 202 positive, six shipments. The standard on-device deployment artifact (`edge/model/edge_model.json`) is fitted on all six after LOSO evaluation. The S2 judge replay uses a separate model (`edge/model/edge_model_s2_loso.json`) fitted only on S1, S3, S4, S5 and S6; S2 was excluded from feature fitting, imputation, scaling and coefficients. This fitted artifact reproduces the independently generated S2 LOSO fold predictions exactly (maximum absolute score difference 0.0). Its score is out-of-sample for shipment S2, but one held-out shipment is not external validation beyond the six-shipment benchmark.

**All-six LOSO evaluation:** six complete-shipment folds; fixed score threshold 0.5; macro F1 `.236`, recall `.441`, PR-AUC `.320`. Pooled across folds: 11/41 onsets covered (26.8%), mean lead 85.5 min, 35.0% HIGH windows, 19 episodes / 9 false under the transitive <=120-minute gap grouping, and 37 / 26 under strict contiguous 10-minute grouping. See `EDGE_MODEL_COMPARISON.md` for precise definitions, thresholds, GPU comparison and row counts. **S2 held-out replay fold:** F1 `.743`, 3/4 onsets covered, mean lead 86.7 min on 188 rows; treat this as one-fold evidence only. Score calibration has not been externally validated.

**Export:** The deployment JSON is 4,224 bytes; the dedicated S2 replay JSON is 4,497 bytes. The JSON includes imputer medians, missing-indicator positions, scaler means/scales, coefficients, intercept, threshold, ordered features, sensor schema and a canonical SHA-256 excluding the self-referential hash field. The all-six Python reference scorer, JavaScript scorer and sklearn agree on 24 real-data vectors to better than `1e-6`; the S2-held-out scorer is additionally checked against its held-out OOF vector set.

**Intended use:** Demonstration of offline on-device thermal-risk early warning on Android Chrome, with human review. **Prohibited:** food safety, shelf life, donation approval, confirmed root cause, automatic refrigeration actuation, or claimed food-waste reduction. This model has no prospective field validation and its current-R2 live gate is not independently reproduced from the upstream benchmark; the replay stops at the recorded severe-state marker to avoid scoring that row.
